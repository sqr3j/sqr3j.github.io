How to make a screenshot in macos

  • 1. Capture the Entire Screen

  • Shortcut: Command + Shift + 3

  • What it does: Instantly takes a picture of everything visible on your screen.

  • Saves to desktop - don't like it very much

  • 2. Save to clipboard

  • Shortcut: Command + Shift + 5

  • What it does: Opens a window like windows snap/snip tool
